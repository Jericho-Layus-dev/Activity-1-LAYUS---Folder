UX Project Proposal:Campus Retro Thrift

User Problem
College students face severe financial constraints, yet clothing is both a daily necessity and a primary medium for self-expression. Many students have gently used, high-quality vintage or thrifted clothes cluttering their dorm closets that they want to liquidate for immediate pocket money. Meanwhile, other students want to shop sustainably and affordably but lack a safe, centralized campus marketplace, forcing them to pay high retail prices or steep online shipping fees.

Target Audience
Student Sellers:Campus residents looking to clear out their wardrobes and instantly convert pre owned fashion into personal pocket money without the hassle of packaging or shipping.
Student Buyers: Eco-conscious and price sensitive students seeking affordable streetwear, vintage gems, and everyday outfits within walking distance of their dormitories.

Chosen Aesthetic Theme
Theme Concept: Vintage Retro Terrace looks
Visual Direction: A warm nostalgic, and organic aesthetic that mirrors an open air weekend flea market. By pairing soft, sun baked apricot backdrops with rich garden leaf green header elements, the marketplace creates an inviting community vibe.
